### 👨🏽‍💻 About

I’m a software engineer and founder focused on turning ideas into real, usable products.

I care about the whole machine: product thinking, interface design, architecture, APIs, databases, infrastructure, deployment and the tiny details users notice when nobody else does.

- Founder & CEO at **[AIO Market Services](https://aiomarketservices.com)**
- Building **[AIOMarketApp](https://www.aiomarketapp.com)**, an all-in-one marketplace spanning commerce, property, food and local services
- Building **KODE**, a coding environment with collaboration designed around real developer workflows
- Building **AIO Launcher**, my personal Android launcher and productivity system
- Working across web, mobile, backend systems and product design
- Most production work lives in private repositories. The commits are shy; the products are not 😄

## 🧠 How I Build

```text
Idea
  ↓
Product thinking
  ↓
Design + architecture
  ↓
Build the smallest real version
  ↓
Test the ugly edges
  ↓
Ship
  ↓
Observe → improve → repeat
```

I like systems that are **simple on the surface and serious underneath**. Good software should feel obvious to use even when the machinery behind it is anything but obvious.
